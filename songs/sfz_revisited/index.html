New instruments, familiar songs

Seventeen free sampled voices, followed by seventeen revised songs. Original scores remain intact. The revisions preserve written notes, harmony, tempo, form, microtonal pitches and binaural settings; instrumentation and faders change.

Use the A/B buttons to switch at the same timestamp. Playback loudness is matched by attenuating the louder master using its measured LUFS. Recorded strings retain their natural vibrato. These are candidates for listening review, not approved improvements.
