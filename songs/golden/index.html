Five Golden Songs

Western harmony is home in all five; golden chords (36-ET, stacked golden thirds) come in as passing light, sparkle, colour, a pivot between keys, and once as a home of their own. Scores and notes are in README.md.

1. Golden Hour Lullaby

C major · 72 BPM · 2:17 · piano, guitar, pad, bass, glass bell
Golden passing chords

A plain lullaby whose chord changes glide through golden chords on the weak beats: C → vFg → F, the escalator F → vF#g → ^F#g → G, and the bridge closes with the four-chord golden authentic cadence G – vE♭T13/G – vCg/G – C. The melody rests or holds a shared note while the piano voices slide by sixth-steps.

2. Deceptive Light

G major · 120 BPM · 2:11 · drums, bass, electric piano, pad, pluck arp, lead
Golden deceptive cadence, escalator, golden modal mixture

A pop song on I–V–vi–IV. Every D → Em passes through vE♭g and E♭g in two quick 8ths, so the bass climbs D – vE♭ – E♭ – E like a walk-up. The pre-chorus climbs the golden escalator into V, the bridge slows the deceptive cadence down so you can hear it, and the ending bends IV through its golden centroid into iv: C – Cg – Cm – G.

3. Tape Bloom

A minor · 90 BPM · 2:01 · swung drums, electric piano, sliding bass, piano melody
Golden colour chords, golden Picardy third

Lo-fi chords that bloom into their golden shadows for half a bar: Fmaj7 → Fg (A sinks to vA, C lifts to ^C, F and E stay), E7 → Eg, a "blue" dominant, and Am → vAg in the drum-less bridge. It reads as tape wow. The coda climbs the third of A from minor through silver and golden to major: Am – Am(^3) – Ag – A.

4. Andalusian Gold

A minor / E Phrygian · 100 BPM · 1:40 · guitar strums, palmas, bass, voice-like lead, bell
Golden Andalusian cadence

The flamenco descent Am – G – F – E, first plain, then golden: Am – A♭g – vA♭g – G – ^F#g – vF#g – F – vF – ^E – E. The bass walks the whole tetrachord down in sixth-steps. The voice sings two beats and the guitar answers in gold; the ending hangs on E.

5. The Silver Third

C → ^E♭ → golden home → C · 90 BPM · 2:07 · piano, pad, golden-spectrum pad and bell, lead, drums
Pivot between layers, golden home, golden giant steps

The theme modulates to ^E♭ major, a "silver third" (333 cents) up, a key that shares no note with C, through the golden chord they share (Fg). Then the Western instruments fall silent and golden-spectrum timbres breathe along the golden circle, until G7 arrives as a visitor and leads home. The finale walks Coltrane's major-third cycle through its golden passing chords: C – Cg – A♭ – A♭g – E – Eg – C.
