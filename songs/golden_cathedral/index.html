The golden ninth, tuning and overtones

Three golden thirds make the seventh; four make the ninth. In 36-ET the ninth is 0, 11, 22, 33, 44 sixth-steps above its root. The editable score spells this as maj9(v3,^5,^^9). The ninth on C is vE♭, rather than an unaltered Western D.

The organ and chimes use exact partial ratios of 2^m × φ^n on tempered fundamentals. The sampled cello, horn and flute retain their recorded acoustic timbres. This is golden harmony in 36-ET, with golden-spectrum colors; it is not an exact-φ tuning of every recorded overtone.
