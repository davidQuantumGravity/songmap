# Epic: one long piece

**[A Comma Higher](a_comma_higher.sm)** (9:48, including a 6 s reverb tail) is a through-composed progressive-metal, modal-jazz and
golden-modal epic in 36-ET. It starts on C and ends on ^C, one sixth-step (33 cents, the 36-ET golden comma) higher.
Between those two points it goes through:
- a golden-pedal intro;
- a 7/8 Phrygian-dominant metal song with verse, 5/4 pre-chorus and chorus;
- a swung modal-jazz head that moves from C dorian to C golden7 (the golden Dorian) and up a comma;
- horn and guitar trading fours through all seven golden7 modes, brightest to darkest;
- the golden jazz cadence home to Cmaj7, then golden giant steps;
- a 7/8 decatonic riff and golden planing, a 13/16 bridge, and a guitar solo round the golden circle;
- a ballad on golden timbres;
- the thirteen-chord comma pump, then the whole metal song again a comma higher.

## How it is built

- **Meters.** Sections set their own meter (`meter="7/8 2+2+3"`, `"5/4 3+2"`, `"13/16 3+3+3+2+2"`); this song
  needed that engine extension. Tempos are 72, 138, 168 (swung) and 69 BPM.
- **Golden modes.** The modes are keys (`key="C golden7_51"`, `golden10_36`). The same deg phrase therefore
  recolours itself in each mode: one note drops by 5 sixth-steps per door. Inside one golden scale no two notes
  are closer than a semitone, so deg lines can't rub against their own chords.
- **Distortion.** The distorted guitar is a patch instrument (`patch axe`: Karplus-Strong string → waveshaper →
  cabinet low-pass) that distorts each note on its own. Golden power chords and planed golden triads keep their
  sixth-step intervals instead of turning into intermodulation mush. Pads are voice-led (`hold`); bass and
  power chords are chord-relative (`rel:`).
- **Drums.** The drums are built from the `v0` voices: a clicky tight kick, three tuned toms, a ride and a crash.
  The jazz feel is written on a 12-step triplet grid, with `swing=.66` on the 8th-note lines.
