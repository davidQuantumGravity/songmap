Index

Every audio example in the book, in chapter order, with the caption that says what to listen for and a player. The short examples were synthesized for this book from SongMap songs or small Python programs whose sources are in textbook/audio/<chapter>/ under the names listed; longer pieces from the golden audio suite appear with their file names. All of them use the stable clean instruments and the clean master (peak at −1 dBFS, no compression), so they are not loudness-matched: listen at a moderate, comfortable level, ideally on headphones or good speakers (About the audio examples). The Chapter column links back to the page where each example is discussed.
