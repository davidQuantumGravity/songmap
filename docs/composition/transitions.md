# Connect two musical states

Describe the last stable phrase before the boundary and the first stable phrase
after it. Record local bar/beat positions, meter, tempo, key, active instruments,
bass note, important melody pitches, dynamics, pickup and release space.

Choose a route with an audible purpose: anticipation, suspension and resolution,
rhythmic fill, subtraction/breath, common-tone bridge, bass connection, or deliberate
direct arrival. More notes and a louder crash are not default answers.

Use the Harmony Library to retrieve candidate chord pairs or progressions. Check
them against the actual melody and duration. A matching root pair does not supply
the correct inversion, voicing, phrase ending or instrumental technique by itself.
For a key change, verify the destination is established and preserve 36-ET when
golden writing is requested.

For a drum fill, author the whole kit's last bar and following downbeat together.
For a golden suspension, decide which voice holds, where it resolves and what
supports it. For a continuous passage, an internal arrival may need only a changed
register, silence or shared-tone handoff rather than an inserted section.

Preview includes the lead-in, transition and enough of the arrival to judge it.
Keep pickup notes and release tails. Label a different-duration proposal as a form
change and update timing maps explicitly; do not force it into an aligned riff A/B.

When the binaural layer is part of the brief, keep its carrier and beat differences
following the actual new harmony with the shared octave reduction. Do not introduce
an independent fixed-Hz rate at a transition. Explain the artistic effect without
promising a neurological result.
